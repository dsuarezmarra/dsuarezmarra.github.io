AutoShorts

AutoShorts is a personal software tool by David Suárez Marra (Spain). It produces short vertical videos (Shorts, Reels and TikToks) in Spanish and English about current topics and evergreen curiosities, and publishes them to the owner's own AutoShorts accounts on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube.

How it works

  • The script is written with an AI language model and fact-checked against the news sources it cites before any video is made.
  • The narration and every shot of footage are generated with AI on the owner's computer. No stock footage and no real people's faces are used. Every upload is labeled as AI-generated content.
  • At most three videos per day and per language are published, at fixed times, after the owner has had the chance to review them.

How it uses TikTok

AutoShorts uses Login Kit so that the owner can authorize it once on each of the owner's TikTok accounts (scopes user.info.basic and video.publish), and the Content Posting API (Direct Post) to upload each finished video with its caption, marked as AI-generated content. It does not read followers, messages, comments or data about other users.

The app is not offered to the public: the only person who signs in to it is the owner.
